About

I work where business, risk and compliance meet. For regulated data products, that means refining and prioritizing the backlog, writing user stories with clear acceptance criteria, and getting business, risk and compliance stakeholders to agree on scope and release priorities across SAFe programs.

My foundation is business analysis: requirements discovery, user acceptance testing and stakeholder sign-off. I stay hands-on with Snowflake, SQL and Power BI, so trade-off conversations with engineering are grounded in the data itself.
